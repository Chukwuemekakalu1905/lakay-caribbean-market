From the islands to Glendale

What you’ll find at Lakay

  • Epis & seasonings

    Green seasoning, creole blends, cloves, and the herbs that start a good stew.

  • Pickles & condiments

    Pikliz, hot sauce, and pickled peppers for griot, tassot, and everyday plates.

  • Grains & flours

    Rice, cornmeal, mayi moulen, and cassava flour for side dishes and breads.

  • Beans & canned goods

    Red beans, pigeon peas, coconut milk, and pantry tins for diri kole and soups.

  • Drinks & treats

    Malta, tropical juices, coconut sweets, and biscuits.

  • Frozen & dried fish

    Herring, salt fish, and freezer staples for weekend cooking.
